## The Conjuring (2013)

*The Conjuring (2013) is a supernatural horror film directed by James Wan, based on the real-life experiences of paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren. The movie follows the Warrens as they help a family plagued by disturbing and violent paranormal activity in their Rhode Island home. It blends chilling suspense with a haunting story, making it one of the most iconic films in the modern horror genre.*
